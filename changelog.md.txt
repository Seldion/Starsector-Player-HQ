1.7.0/1 Change Logs

Fixed a few small issues. (1.7.1)

Removed 2 Mining Fleets and added 2 Salvage Fleets to the MAH, They will launch and search for debris fields and derelict ships, they will ignore ships with guards.

Added a Winter Resort to the Resort.
VR Suites now give a bonus % based off the hazard rating of the planet over 100%.
Beach Resort now gives a bonus for cold, very cold and planets with no atmosphere that are also cold.
Winter Resort give bonuses to hot and very hot planets.

Fixed where you can now produce fighters at the HQ.

Reworked the Courier's mechanic. (Forgot to actually add it but made a better version.)
Couriers will now return to the HQ as soon as the screen is closed.
There is now a Charter option on the main menu that allows you to set supply colonies and request colonies for the courier.

If the player has the automated ship skill they can use automated ships in the patrol HQ.

Added a Shopping List feature that allows your trade ship to buy equipment and ships from the market they are visiting.

Fixed the Exchange offering things it shouldn't offer for both the weapons and the ship side.

Fixed Expeditions returning with things they shouldn't like models of ships and stations.

Reworked the mining fleets, they now launch when ready, they have a 4 day cooldown after each mining trip, they will now travel to a planet and mine, each day they add to the fleets total cargo hold and when full will return to the station, if the station's hold is full of the resources the system offers the fleets will hold off on launching until the hold goes lower then 8,000.

1.3 Change Logs

Military Paradigm: Reworked the Expedition HQ now named Fleet HQ with 3 different fleet types, Expedition, Logistics and Patrol. Expedition works like it use to, Patrol allows you to build custom fleets to patrol the sector by building the ships and then adding them to the fleet, Logistics has two different things, Resupply Bay and Courier bays, Resupply allows you to field a fleet that when you use the ability it will travel to your location and allow you to get supplies as long as you have them in your HQ, the courier fleets will allow you to transport stuff between your colonies and the HQ. Fleets produced by the Patrol aren't lost when destroyed, when fully destroyed it will take a full month for them to redeploy, 3 days for each frigate replaced, 6 for destroyers, 10 for cruisers and 16 for capitals. To build Expedition fleets, there will need to be a planet with ruins on them in the system. Added the ability to "inject" credits into the custom production but at 10x the price.

Manufactorium: In the Manufactorium you can now pick between a Corrupt and Pristine, the corrupt doubles the cost of production. Removed the Fusion Core(remove before updating.) and gave the bonuses to Catalytic Core.

Materials Acquisition Hub: Reworked the Materials Acquisition Hub, removed the installable items, so remove them before updating, reduced the mining scope to only the planet/belt the station is located at and added mining fleets. Improved resource harvest % from 15% to 20%
